Party Management

Build Your Party Registry

Before creating an election survey, you must first register the political parties that will appear as voting options. The Party Registry is private to your account — no other user can view or modify your parties.

account_balance
Step 1

Create a New Party

Navigate to the Party section from the top navigation bar and click + Add Party. A dialog will appear prompting you to fill in the following details:

  • label
    Party Name — The official name of the political party (required field).
  • person
    Party Leader — The name of the party's designated leader (optional, but recommended for clarity).
  • palette
    Party Colour — Choose from a predefined palette or enter a custom hex code. This colour is used throughout charts and result displays.
  • image
    Party Logo (optional) — Upload a logo using one of three methods: Local File (drag-and-drop or browse, max 2 MB), Online URL (paste any publicly accessible image URL), or Google Drive (paste a shareable Drive link).
info Once a logo URL is entered, the system previews the image immediately. A green confirmation message confirms successful loading before you save.
Add New Party dialog — fill in details and upload a logo
Step 2

View and Manage Your Parties

After saving, the party appears as a card on the Party Management page. Each card displays the party name, leader, logo (or generated initial), and assigned colour. Two action icons at the bottom of each card allow you to:

  • edit
    Edit — Modify the party's name, leader, colour, or logo at any time. Edits apply to all future surveys that include this party.
  • delete
    Delete — Permanently remove the party from your registry. Note: removing a party that is already associated with a published survey may affect result displays.
lightbulb Use the Search bar at the top of the page to quickly locate a specific party when your registry contains many entries.
Party Management page — all registered parties shown as cards
02
Survey Management

Create, Publish & Manage Surveys

A survey is the central unit of Voter-Pulse. It defines the election context, the competing parties, the voting window, and controls when the public can participate. Only authenticated users may create and manage surveys.

poll
Step 1

Create a New Election Survey

Click Create Survey in the top navigation. The survey creation form requires the following information:

  • title
    Survey Name — A clear, descriptive title for the election survey (e.g., "State Election Survey 2026").
  • description
    Description (optional) — Additional context about the survey displayed to participants on the voting page.
  • date_range
    Start & End Date — Define the active voting window. Votes can only be submitted within this date range.
  • shield
    Anonymous Survey — Enable this option to keep all voter identities private. The organiser will only see aggregated results.
  • groups
    Select Parties — Choose at least two parties from your Party Registry to appear as voting options. Selected parties are shown as colour-coded chips at the bottom for easy review.
Survey creation form — define details and select competing parties
Step 2

Review Your Survey in Draft Mode

Upon saving, the survey is created in Draft status and you are automatically redirected to the Survey Detail page. In Draft mode:

  • visibility_off
    The survey is not publicly accessible. No votes can be cast.
  • edit_note
    All survey fields remain fully editable. Use the Edit button to make changes before going live.
  • rocket_launch
    When you are ready to go live, click Publish Survey to make the survey accessible to the public.
warning Once a survey is published, the party selection and core settings are locked and cannot be edited. Review all details carefully before publishing.
Survey detail page in Draft mode — review, edit, or publish
Step 3

Publish the Survey

Clicking Publish Survey triggers a confirmation dialog to ensure intentional publishing. Click OK to confirm — the system will immediately:

  • check_circleUpdate the survey status to Published
  • check_circleGenerate a unique, shareable survey URL
  • check_circleMake the survey available for public voting
  • check_circleReplace the action bar with Copy Link, Deactivate, and Close Survey controls
Publish confirmation dialog
Survey published — unique URL generated
Step 4

Share the Survey Link

Once published, the platform automatically dispatches a beautifully formatted invitation email containing:

  • email
    The survey title and organiser name
  • link
    A prominent Cast Your Vote Now button that links directly to the voting page
  • content_copy
    The full survey URL for copy-paste sharing across WhatsApp, social media, or any messaging platform

You can also manually share the URL displayed on the Survey Detail page by clicking the Copy Survey Link button.

Invitation email sent to participants upon survey publication
03
Vote Management

Cast, Verify & Monitor Votes

Voters do not need a Voter-Pulse account to participate. The platform uses email-based OTP verification to ensure each participant casts exactly one vote per survey, maintaining the integrity of results.

how_to_vote
Step 1

Access the Voting Page

When a participant opens the survey link, they are presented with the Voting Page. No login or registration is required. Participants complete the following fields:

  • email
    Email Address (required) — Used solely to send the one-time verification code. Not shared with the organiser in anonymous surveys.
  • people
    Demographic Information (optional) — Gender, age, and location. These fields help organisers understand voting patterns across demographics.
  • how_to_vote
    Party Selection — Choose the preferred party from the list of participating parties. The selected party is highlighted and a preview appears at the bottom of the page.
Voting page — accessible to the public without any login
Step 2

Receive the One-Time Verification Code

Upon submitting the vote, the system dispatches a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) to the participant's email address. The verification email clearly displays:

  • pin
    The 6-digit verification code in large, individually-boxed digits for easy reading.
  • timer
    The expiry window (5 minutes) — the code must be used promptly to confirm the vote.
  • check_box
    The party selection summary, confirming which party the vote will be recorded for.
security If you did not attempt to vote, simply ignore the email. Your vote will not be recorded unless the code is entered and submitted.
Verification email containing the 6-digit OTP code
Step 3

Enter the Code & Confirm Your Vote

Return to the survey page where an OTP verification dialog is displayed. Enter the 6-digit code received in your email:

  • keyboard
    Type each digit into the individual input boxes. The dialog auto-advances to the next box as each digit is entered.
  • timer
    A countdown timer displays the remaining validity of the code. Click Resend if the code has expired.
  • verified
    Click Verify & Submit Vote to confirm. The vote is permanently recorded once verified and cannot be changed.
OTP dialog — enter the code and click Verify & Submit Vote
Step 4

Vote Confirmed — Thank You Page

Upon successful verification, the participant is directed to a confirmation page that:

  • check_circle
    Confirms the vote has been recorded and verified successfully
  • ballot
    Displays the party voted for as a clear summary card
  • info
    Notes that results will be published once the survey officially closes
verified_user Each email address is permitted to cast exactly one vote per survey. Any subsequent attempt using the same email will be rejected by the system.
Vote confirmation page — the participant's choice is recorded
Step 5

Monitor Live Results on the Dashboard

Authenticated organisers can track the public's voting activity in real time from the Survey Analytics Dashboard. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of results including:

  • bar_chart
    KPI summary cards — total votes cast, number of participating parties, overall participation rate, and the currently leading party.
  • poll
    Votes by Party chart — a horizontal bar chart displaying each party's vote count and percentage share.
  • donut_large
    Gender Distribution donut chart — a breakdown of votes by gender (Male, Female, Other) across the entire survey.
  • stacked_bar_chart
    Gender Breakdown by Party — a stacked bar chart revealing the gender composition of each party's vote share for deeper demographic analysis.
Analytics Dashboard — real-time results, charts, and demographic insights
